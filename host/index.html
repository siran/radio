The queue — what is going to play, in order, once the track on the air finishes
The queue is what actually plays next. It lives inside liquidsoap and not in a file, so it does not survive a restart, and a track leaves it the moment it starts playing. A playlist, in the panel below, is a list you keep and plays nothing until you send it here.
    Find a track — search the library, then put what you find in the queue or in a playlist
    Searching by itself changes nothing and reaches nobody. A result goes where the Add to box says: the queue plays it, a playlist only keeps it.
      Playlists — lists you keep, saved on the server under a name
      A playlist is a list you keep, saved on the server in your own folder. It does not play. The queue, in its own panel above, is what is going to play, in that order, once what is on the air now finishes. Sending a playlist to the queue copies the tracks across, so changing the playlist afterwards does not change what will play.
        Downloading takes the playlist selected above. Uploading writes the tracks in the file into the playlist selected above, replacing what was in it — the name inside the file is ignored, no other playlist changes, and nothing reaches the air until you press Send to the queue.
        Equalizer — how loud you are, and what shape. Applies to your notes only, on the radio, after they arrive.
        whether the music drops under your notes, or plays on behind them
        The narrator — which voice announces the tracks, how fast, how often, and how it says a name it keeps getting wrong. One setting for the whole station.
        Mixing desk — the station's own knobs, live for every listener. The compressor sits on the voice bus, so it shapes voice notes and the live mic together; the music never passes through it. Read when this fold is opened.
        Streaming — everything this phone does to your voice before it is sent
        Kept on this device, like the microphone above. next note lands on the next thing you record; mic reopen needs Stop talking, then Start talking.

        Presets — both halves of your sound, saved on the server under a name
        Your voice and your streaming settings together, saved in your own folder on the server — not on this phone, so clearing your browser cannot lose them. Loading one puts your voice on the air within a quarter second; bitrate and bitrate mode land on the next note; everything else on Streaming needs a mic reopen.
        Downloading takes the sound selected above. Uploading saves the one in the file under the name in the box, replacing only that one — nothing else you have saved changes, and nothing reaches the air until you press Load.
